# Contributor Covenant & AI Ethics Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## 🤖 AI Ethical Guidelines (Project Specific)

The Talent Intelligence Agent is an extremely powerful tool. Because it calculates human "Burnout Risk" and "Performance Grades" using analytical algorithms and Generative AI, all contributors MUST adhere to strict ethical data standards:

1. **Anti-Bias Commitment**: Any new mathematical weight added to the Coral engine's SQL queries must not disproportionately penalize engineers for factors outside their control. We do not merge PRs that rely solely on "Lines of Code" to judge engineering value.
2. **Metadata Only**: We strictly prohibit any Pull Request that attempts to scrape, store, or analyze the *content* of private Slack messages or private Calendar invites. The agent must only process quantitative metadata (timestamps, message counts, response latency).
3. **Empathetic AI Architecture**: Contributions to the Gemini prompt engineering must maintain a tone of empathy, support, and constructive management. The AI should never be engineered to automate punitive HR actions, disciplinary warnings, or firings. The goal is to detect burnout and help the employee.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
* Using the AI codebase to build unauthorized surveillance tools.
* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at [security@yourdomain.com]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
